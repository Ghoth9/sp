# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Admins and data entry staff keying in customer and service data on desktop computers, and air conditioner technicians viewing, logging services, and checking appointments on Android and iOS mobile devices while working on-site.

## Product Purpose
A responsive, offline-first customer and service management application for air conditioning maintenance that ensures clean data entry on desktop, seamless work tracking (with photos) on mobile, and reliable appointment scheduling.

## Brand Personality
Crisp, clean, professional, and trustworthy. Anchored in a high-contrast white layout with a premium slate blue accent.

## Anti-references
Low-contrast text, SaaS-cream/beige background templates (claude-beige), decorative gradients, overcrowded dialogs, and non-standard custom controls that slow down keyboard or mobile flow.

## Design Principles
- **Utility First**: Design for readability and fast interaction, prioritizing clean grids and tables on desktop, and touch-friendly cards on mobile.
- **Familiar Patterns**: Use standard components (native buttons, forms, modals) so the interface disappears into the task.
- **Outdoor Legibility**: Text contrast must exceed WCAG AA standards (7:1 where possible) to ensure technicians can read the UI under direct sunlight on site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. High contrast text, explicit focus states for keyboard navigation, and full support for reduced motion preferences.
